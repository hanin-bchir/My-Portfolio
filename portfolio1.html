About Me

I'm a passionate engineering preparatory student and co-founder of NextVerse, driven by the intersection of technology, creativity, and innovation. My journey spans game development, AI systems, digital marketing, and web development — always with a focus on building experiences that matter.

NextVerse is a student-founded initiative created by two passionate IT students with a vision to go beyond the classroom. We see technology as more than a field of study — it's our playground for creativity, innovation, and problem-solving. From game development and software projects to hackathons and collaborations, we are documenting our journey as student founders shaping the digital future. At NextVerse, being students is not a limitation, it's our strength.

Through hands-on projects and collaborative work, I've developed expertise in low-level programming with C and SDL, built intelligent systems using Python and computer vision concepts, and crafted compelling digital content that drives real business results. I hold certifications in Python from GoMyCode and C and Python from IEEE.

As an active IEEE member, I contribute to workshops, hackathons, and community events, fostering knowledge sharing and hands-on learning. I've had the privilege of organizing bootcamps and leading technical initiatives that empower fellow students in engineering and technology.

Beyond coding, I bring a creative edge through videography, trailer editing, and social media strategy — skills that helped me double a client's sales in one week and win national awards for game trailers.

I'm currently seeking opportunities to collaborate on innovative projects where technology meets creativity, whether it's building intelligent systems, crafting immersive experiences, or driving digital transformation.
