IEEE SIGHT Ambassador

Technology That Serves People

Being selected as an IEEE SIGHT (Special Interest Group on Humanitarian Technology) Ambassador in collaboration with UNESCO represents a commitment to using technology as a tool for positive societal impact. Through this role, I engage in initiatives that aim to address real-world challenges by combining engineering, innovation, and community-driven solutions.

IEEE SIGHT is a global initiative that focuses on applying technology to solve humanitarian challenges, particularly in underserved communities. It encourages engineers and students to design solutions that improve quality of life and promote sustainable development.

I completed SIGHT Ambassador Mission 0 — a multi-platform awareness campaign — and represented the branch at the SIGHT Day Congress with the IEEE Tunisia Section.
